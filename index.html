Hackathons

Team projects built against the clock, from a hospital’s documentation to a university’s heating bill.

  • STU Ideathon

    3rd place

    Slovak University of TechnologyRandomly formed team

    A digital twin that finds where university heating is wasted

    Our faculties spend hundreds of thousands of euros a year on heating. We proposed a sensor network that collects data over a measurement period, builds a digital twin of the buildings and uses it to find where heat is lost or spent at the wrong time.

    • Count body heat: each person in a room gives off around 100 W, so a full lecture hall can be pre-heated about 1 °C lower.
    • Heat by the timetable: warm a room for the classes actually scheduled in it, not on a fixed clock.
    • Shift heating to the night where the building can store it.
    • Heat only the rooms in use at a given time, not the whole building.

About me

I study Information and Communication Technologies at STU FEI in Bratislava and Computer Science in parallel at Dnipro University of Technology.

I like knowing what happens underneath an interface, so my projects go deep in different directions: a mesh-routing protocol with 167 passing tests, a Verilog decoder, Java backends with JWT and WebSockets, and Python agents built on LLM APIs.

Right now I’m building Python agents on top of GPT and Claude APIs, tools for evaluating LLM output, and n8n automation pipelines. I also team up at hackathons: RAKATHON 2026, Huawei Seeds for the Future and the STU Ideathon, where my team placed 3rd.
