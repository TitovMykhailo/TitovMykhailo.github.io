A role-based clinic application for patient bookings, doctor schedules, administration and appointment-linked messaging.

The problem

A booking involves three parties: a patient choosing a time, a doctor with a schedule, and staff who oversee everything. Messages about the visit usually end up in a separate channel, disconnected from the booking itself.

What I built

  • Patient, doctor and admin roles, each with its own pages and permissions enforced by Spring Security and JWT.
  • Free slots generated from each doctor’s weekly schedule, with conflict checks before a booking is saved.
  • Every booking automatically creates a chat thread and a first message from the doctor’s template ({doctorName}, {date}, {time}…).
  • Realtime chat over STOMP / SockJS with a REST fallback, plus Swagger docs and service tests for auth, doctors and appointments.

What I learned

Keeping booking rules on the backend — slot generation, conflict checks, role checks — kept the three interfaces simple and consistent.
