An AI-assisted editing workflow packaged as a Claude Code skill: transcription, silence and filler trimming, captions, clip and sound selection, colour grade and a DaVinci Resolve timeline.

The problem

Turning a narration into a fast-cut, captioned video takes dozens of small, repetitive editing decisions.

What I built

  • Transcription and cut planning that trims silence and filler words without cutting a word in half.
  • Synced captions, clip and sound-effect selection from a local media library, with generated frames as a fallback.
  • Two style profiles (faceless visual essay, talking-head/screen demo) with overlays for one-off tweaks.
  • DaVinci Resolve automation for tracks, captions, grade and render, plus timeline validation and a local dashboard for parallel builds.

Results

Public repository with an environment checker and setup guide. Full operation needs DaVinci Resolve and a local media library.

What I learned

Visible intermediate files make an automated pipeline editable: you can inspect and fix the plan before anything is rendered.
