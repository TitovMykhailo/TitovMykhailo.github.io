A team hackathon prototype built at RAKATHON 2026 in Brno: it proposes structured values from pathology documents and tumour diagrams, and shows them next to the source for a clinician to confirm before registry export.

The problem

Information in pathology reports and tumour diagrams has to be copied into consistent registry fields by hand. The challenge from the Masaryk Memorial Cancer Institute was to speed this up without taking the clinician out of the loop.

What I built

  • A document → proposed record workflow: values are extracted and suggested, never written to the registry automatically.
  • A review screen that shows each proposed value next to the passage it came from.
  • Explicit handling of corrections and conflicting information before a record is confirmed.
  • An architecture meant for hospital-controlled servers, so documents are processed locally.
