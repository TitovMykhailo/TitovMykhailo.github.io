Built by our team at RAKATHON 2026 in Brno, billed as the world’s largest AI hackathon against cancer. A urologist treating prostate cancer needed a tool that reads his records, compares them, fills the hospital’s REDCap documentation — and points out errors and contradictions in the records.

The problem

A urologist treating prostate cancer copies information from his own reports, notes and diagrams into the hospital’s REDCap registry by hand. It takes time, and mistakes in the source records travel straight into the database.

What I built

  • Reads the doctor’s records — reports, notes and tumour diagrams — and extracts the value each REDCap field needs.
  • Compares the documents with each other, so every value is checked against all of its sources.
  • Flags errors and contradictions in the records and shows the doctor exactly where they are. This was the key feature.
  • Fills the REDCap form as a proposal: nothing is saved until the doctor reviews and confirms it.
  • Designed to run on hospital-controlled servers, so documents never leave the institution.

Team prototype presented at RAKATHON 2026 in Brno. Using it on real patient records would need clinical validation and an approved hospital deployment.

What I learned

In medical documentation, catching mistakes in the source records matters as much as filling the form — and the doctor has to stay in control of every value.
