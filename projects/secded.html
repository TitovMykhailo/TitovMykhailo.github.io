The problem.

A communication channel can change a transmitted bit. This research connects the theory of error-correcting codes with an HDL implementation and an evaluation setup for comparing decoding approaches.

Inside the project.

  • An extended Hamming (8,4) SEC-DED encoder and decoder in Verilog.
  • A custom bit layout linking syndrome values to bit positions.
  • Reliability checks and hybrid correction logic.
  • A BPSK + AWGN simulation setup for studying BER across an Eb/N0 sweep.

How it fits together.

04Compare errors

The emphasis is on understanding where the decoder succeeds, where it fails and how to demonstrate correctness through testbenches and controlled experiments.

Current state.

Research in progress. The source has not been linked publicly, and measured BER results are not published on this page. The bit graphic is an explanatory illustration, not a benchmark.
