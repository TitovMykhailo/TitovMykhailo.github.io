Extended Hamming (8,4) encoding and decoding in Verilog, reliability-based hybrid correction logic, and a BPSK + AWGN simulation setup for comparing hard and hybrid decoding.

The problem

A noisy channel can flip transmitted bits. This research connects the theory of error-correcting codes with a hardware implementation and an experiment that compares decoding strategies.

What I built

  • An extended Hamming (8,4) SEC-DED encoder and decoder written in Verilog.
  • A custom bit layout that maps syndrome values directly to bit positions.
  • Reliability checks and hybrid correction logic on top of hard-decision decoding.
  • A BPSK + AWGN simulation for measuring bit-error rate across an Eb/N0 sweep.

How it works
